ABOUT

About the project

A fictional Yamato–Iowa engagement made in collaboration with GPT Astra. The human creator proposed the features and details, from ship models and turret animations to ballistics and damage behavior.

The models draw on historical specifications and layouts. Ballistics, armor and damage calculations use approximations designed for a game.
